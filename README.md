# GALACTIC WAR BETWEEN ALIENS??!! 0w0
A revival of the classic computer game Pong, this time in space! Also, you can turn the paddles. (revolutionary)
A few screenshots of the game can be found in the screenshots folder.

This game was created for Haven Hong Kong 2026 using Unity and C#, in cooperation with my good friend and talented artist Venus. You can play the game for free at https://hepialidae.itch.io/galactic-war-between-aliens-0w0.

No AI was used for this project. I am committed to keeping my games and the gamedev industry free of AI, as it should be.

## Development Details
I made the main parts of the game-the spaceship selection screen and the ping pong game-in two days. I planned to make a simple but polished game for the game jam, but due to my unfamiliarity with Unity physics, there were a lot of physics-related bugs. 

After the jam, I fixed the physics bugs, re-made the colliders of the spaceships, and added some extra power-ups! I also added a restart button, for anyone who wants to play the game over and over again, because reloading doesn't feel very good.
